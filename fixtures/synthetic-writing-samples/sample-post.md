# The dashboard was innocent. The workflow was not.

A team asked for a cleaner dashboard because their monthly numbers kept drifting.

The charts were ugly, but they were not the real problem. Three systems defined the same status differently, and each team had built a local workaround around its preferred definition.

We stopped polishing the disagreement and mapped the workflow instead. Once the handoffs and definitions were visible, the reporting problem became fairly ordinary.

Analytics is useful when it helps you find the broken logic underneath the numbers. Otherwise it is just expensive wallpaper.
